How WapeWape Works

We buy in bulk directly from trusted suppliers and manufacturers, which means we get wholesale pricing — and we pass that discount straight to you. Every deal you see has a real "was" price and a real "now" price, verified before it's ever posted.

Only one product carries a discount at a time: today's Deal of the Day. Everything else in the shop is sold at an honest, fixed price — no inflated "was" prices to fake a bigger discount.

Payment & Delivery

Checkout works through WhatsApp — you place your order on the site, choose M-Pesa or pay-on-delivery, and we confirm everything with you directly before any money changes hands.

  • M-Pesa and card payments accepted
  • Pay-on-delivery available in select areas
  • Delivery covers all of Kenya

Returns & Refunds

If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with a photo, and we'll sort out a replacement or refund. See our full Return Policy for details.

Trust & Verification

Every deal — whether it's our own stock or a seller's submission through the Sell With WapeWape portal — goes through the same check before it's allowed to go live: is the price genuine, is the stock real, and does it meet our quality bar.
